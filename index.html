About Us

Creative instinct,
grounded in engineering.

Casa means home — a place of comfort, warmth and memories. Render means transforming ideas into visible, executable design. Together, Casa Renders brings interior-design creativity and structural-engineering expertise under one integrated consultancy.

Built on a family legacy in engineering and project execution dating back to 1988, the practice combines creative planning, technical understanding and practical coordination to deliver functional, elegant and future-ready spaces.

Jai Sirsiya has been actively working in structural engineering since 2018.

We support residential, commercial and hospitality projects across India, collaborating with architects, contractors, builders, manufacturers and interior firms to deliver cohesive project outcomes.

Our process

From design
to move-in.

Eight transparent stages keep creativity, budget, technical inputs and execution moving toward one resolved outcome.

01Meet your designer+

Share the space, goals, timeline and budget expectations.

The first conversation identifies priorities, project constraints and the right level of support.
02Site and requirement study+

We review measurements, drawings, site conditions and practical needs.

A clear starting point reduces late-stage changes and creates a reliable design brief.
03Space planning+

Layouts, circulation, storage and furniture relationships are resolved.

Every decision is tested against how the space will actually be used day to day.
04Design and visualisation+

Materials, lighting, colours and key 3D views bring the direction to life.

Visual clarity helps approve the space before execution begins.
05Technical coordination+

Drawings, structural inputs and execution details are aligned.

Interior ambition and technical reality are coordinated within one project conversation.
06Material and budget freeze+

The selected scope, finishes, quotation and project plan are confirmed.

A transparent freeze point protects the budget and reduces hidden surprises.
07Execution and quality review+

Site work, vendors and design compliance are coordinated as per package.

Progress is reviewed against the approved design and agreed deliverables.
08Move-in and handover+

Final reviews, styling guidance and project closure bring the space together.

The journey ends with a functional, finished environment ready to be lived in.
